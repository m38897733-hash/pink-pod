PINK POD - how to get your APK
1. Make a free account at github.com and create a new repository (e.g. pinkpod).
2. Upload ALL files/folders from this zip (keep the hidden .github folder!).
3. Open the "Actions" tab > "Build APK" > wait ~5 minutes until it turns green.
4. Open that run > Artifacts > download "PinkPod-APK" > unzip > app-debug.apk.
5. Send the APK to your phone, tap it, allow "install unknown apps".
6. Open Pink Pod > tap "Allow music access" > switch Pink Pod ON. Play a song in Spotify - done!
